What happens next

  1. Stock is checked

    If the shelf covers the request, it is fulfilled at once and stock goes down.

  2. Donors are alerted

    If not, whatever is on the shelf is released, the request stays pending, and compatible donors are emailed.

  3. Donations are recorded

    Donations logged against the request count toward it.
