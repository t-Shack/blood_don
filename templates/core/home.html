How BDMIS works

No paperwork and no cost. You register once, and the system does the matching.

  1. Register your blood type

    Tell us your ABO group and rhesus factor. We work out every patient you are compatible with.

  2. Get an alert when it matters

    When a hospital runs short of blood you can give, you get one email with the hospital, the urgency and the units needed.

  3. Donate, and we count the days

    After you give, BDMIS holds you out for {{ DONATION_INTERVAL_DAYS }} days and shows the exact date you can donate again.

Can you donate?

Most healthy adults aged 18 to 65 can. The blood bank screens everyone on the day.
