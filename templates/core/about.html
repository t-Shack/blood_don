About BDMIS

A blood donor management information system built for Nigerian hospitals.

The problem

Nigerian hospitals routinely run short of specific blood types with no reliable way to reach the donors who could help. Donor records sit in paper ledgers, stock levels are known only to the blood bank, and appeals go out through informal channels.

What BDMIS does

It keeps a live register of donors and their blood types, and tracks stock per hospital for each blood type. The moment a request cannot be met from stock, it emails every eligible, compatible donor with the hospital, the urgency and the shortfall.

Compatibility, exactly

Matching uses strict ABO and rhesus rules. An O− donor can give to anyone, and an AB+ patient can receive from anyone. Every pairing in between follows the same table the blood bank would use, and automated tests cover all of it. This chart is drawn from that table.
